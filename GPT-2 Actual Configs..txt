Here , we are discussing and explaining in brief about the actual dataset and model configurations used in GPT-2

1. The actual GPT-2 Dataset: WebText

GPT-2 was trained on a dataset called WebText,
created specifically for the project. The motivation was to get a large, diverse collection of reasonably high-quality
Internet text rather than simply using Common Crawl.

How WebText was collected

The process was roughly:

Reddit → links → web pages → extracted text → cleaning/deduplication → WebText

OpenAI used Reddit outbound links that had received at least 3 karma as a quality filter. 
The reasoning was that Reddit users had effectively provided a weak form of human curation:
if people upvoted a link, it was more likely to be interesting, useful, educational, etc.


2. GPT-2 Architecture

GPT-2 is a Transformer Decoder-style Architecture

It was actually a scaled up version of original GPT architecture

important components include:

Input text
     ↓
Byte-level BPE tokenization
     ↓
Token embeddings
     +
Positional information
     ↓
Transformer blocks
     ↓
Self-attention + feed-forward networks
     ↓
Vocabulary probabilities
     ↓
Next-token prediction


The model uses masked self-attention, meaning when predicting token \(t\), it cannot look at future tokens.

For example:

The cat sat on the mat

  ↓
  ↓
  ↓
  ↓

When predicting "mat", it can see:

" The cat sat on the "

but not "mat" itself or anything after it.

    

Gpt 2 Small had 117M total 

12 Transformer Layers

each token has 768 embedding dimensions

======================================

| Model        | Parameters | Layers | Hidden size | Attention heads |
| ------------ | ---------: | -----: | ----------: | --------------: |
| GPT-2 Small  |   **117M** |     12 |         768 |              12 |
| GPT-2 Medium |   **345M** |     24 |        1024 |              16 |
| GPT-2 Large  |   **762M** |     36 |        1280 |              20 |
| GPT-2 XL     |   **1.5B** |     48 |        1600 |              25 |


These are often referred to as approximately:

124M / 355M / 774M / 1.5B in later releases/documentation, while the original paper reports
approximately 117M / 345M / 762M / 1.5B depending on parameter-count conventions. OpenAI's staged release
described the publicly released models as 117M, 345M, 762M and eventually 1.5B.


What changes as the model gets bigger?

Basically three major dimensions increase:

1. Number of Transformer layers

12 → 24 → 36 → 48

2. Hidden/embedding dimensionality

768 → 1024 → 1280 → 1600

3. Number of attention heads

12 → 16 → 20 → 25

So GPT-2 isn't four completely different architectures.

It is essentially:

the same Transformer design scaled upward.

This is much shorter than the context windows of modern LLMs they are having in millions now a days

6. Tokenization

GPT-2 did something particularly important with tokenization.

It used byte-level Byte Pair Encoding (BPE).

Instead of requiring every word to be represented as one token,
text is broken into subword/byte-level units.


7. Training configuration

The central training setup was:

Objective

Causal language modeling / next-token prediction

Input:
The stock market crashed because

Target:
investors

The model learns to maximize the probability of the correct next token.

Training data
WebText
≈ 8 million documents
≈ 40 GB text


Training style

Unsupervised/self-supervised learning

There were no human-written labels such as:

Question → Answer
Sentiment → Positive
Article → Summary

The text itself provides the training signal.

For example:

The company reported record profits

can automatically produce training examples:

The → company
The company → reported
The company reported → record
The company reported record → profits

This is why it is more accurately called self-supervised today:
the training targets come from the text itself.
